Manifesto

Substance is style. People, not programs.

  • The software is a gift. The value is the service.

    Every tool is open source and nothing is held back. Self-hosters get everything the managed service has. When you want help running it, you work with the people who built it.

  • Your team first.

    We design from the bottom up, starting with the people who do the work every day. Your team shapes its own programmes, forms and stages, without waiting for a developer.

  • People decide.

    Fairfold never rejects an application, awards a grant or makes a payment on its own. A named person does, and the record shows who.

  • Applicants hear when you are ready.

    Recording a decision and telling the applicant are separate steps, so nobody learns an outcome early.

  • Your data, whenever and wherever. No tie-in.

    Take your data out whenever you like, in open formats including the 360Giving Data Standard. Run Fairfold where you choose, on your own servers or with UK and EU providers. It runs on standard PostgreSQL, with no feature tied to one cloud.

  • One tool or many.

    Use a single tool, or switch on several. They share one record of the organisations and people you work with, so you enter each fact once.

  • Safe by design.

    The database itself keeps each organisation's records apart, and everything that matters goes in an audit log.

  • Built for everyone.

    Every screen works with a keyboard and a screen reader and meets WCAG 2.2 AA.

  • Honest about where we are.

    We say what works and what does not yet, and we publish our plans. Real data waits for an independent penetration test and accessibility audit.

Timeline

  1. Oct 2026

    A full funding round Now

    Fairfold Grants from set-up to release and export, on synthetic data.

  2. Nov to Dec 2026

    The shared platform

    Workflow, documents and e-signature, notifications with consent, permissions and search.

  3. Early 2027

    Independent testing

    A penetration test, Cyber Essentials Plus and an accessibility audit before any real data.

  4. Mar 2027

    First live round

    Grants goes live, with CRM, Due Diligence, Forms, Data Protection and Reporting.

  5. Jun 2027

    Running the money

    Payments, Impact, Governance, Communications, Integrations and more programme types.

  6. Sep 2027

    Beyond grants

    Activities, Fund Accounting, Recruitment, Volunteers, the Field App and the Bid Manager.

  7. Dec 2027

    Every tool launched

    Fundraising, Case Management, Membership, the Assistant, the Funding Map and the Organisation Profile.

These are plans, and dates can move. The repository says what each tool will do.
