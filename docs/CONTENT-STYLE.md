# Content style guide

Words in the interface exist to help people get their task done.

## Voice

- Plain English. Aim for a reading age of about 11 in the applicant portal.
- Sentence case everywhere, including buttons and headings.
- Active voice, plain verbs. Address the user as "you".
- Name things by what users understand, not how the system is built
  ("Reviewers", not "Assignment pool entities").

## Buttons and actions

- Say exactly what happens: "Submit application", "Release decisions",
  "Save and continue". Never "Submit" or "OK" alone.
- Keep the same name through the flow: a "Release decisions" button leads to
  a "Decisions released" confirmation.

## Errors

- Say what went wrong and how to fix it: "Enter a date after 1 April 2027".
- Do not apologise, blame the user, or use codes as the only explanation.
- Put the message next to the field, and summarise at the top of long forms.

## Empty states

- Tell the user what to do next: "No applications yet. Share the programme
  link to start receiving applications."

## Applicants

- Applying for money is stressful. Be warm and precise.
- Tell people how long something will take and what they need before they
  start.
- Explain decisions and next steps clearly. Never hint at an outcome before it
  is released.

## Numbers, dates and money

- Dates: 1 April 2027. Times: 5pm (with timezone when it matters for a deadline).
- Money: £25,000. No pence unless needed.
