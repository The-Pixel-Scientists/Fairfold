# Retention and subject access

This page says how long Fairfold Grants keeps data, and what to do when a
person asks what you hold about them. Both are mostly handled by the operator
today. There are no screens for them yet.

The page has fixed headings, so steps can be added under each one as screens
arrive.

## How long data is kept

Every field the software stores is registered with a sensitivity and a
retention rule. The rule says how long the data is kept and what happens at
the end.

- **Audit events** are kept for at least 12 months. This is a platform minimum.
  No funder can set it lower, and only a software release can.
- **Everything else** follows the rule registered for it.

What the software does today:

- Retention rules are set by the software, not by you. A retention setting is
  not yet available.
- Nothing is deleted when its retention time passes. A routine that removes
  expired data is not yet available. Keep this in mind when you write your own
  retention policy: you are responsible for what you tell people.

## When a person asks for their data

A person can ask what you hold about them. This is a subject access request.
There is a legal deadline, so act the day it arrives.

There is no subject access screen yet. The software cannot yet gather
everything about one person for you. A subject access screen is not yet
available.

### What to do today

1. Write down who asked, what they asked for, and the date you received it.
2. Check who is asking. Do not release anything to someone you have not
   checked.
3. Ask the operator to help you find what is held about that person. Give
   them the person's name and email address, and your deadline. No tool does
   this yet, so it is done by hand and takes time.
4. Check what you have gathered before you send it. It may name other
   people.
5. Keep a record of what you sent and when.

## When a person asks you to erase their data

Erasure is not yet available, as a screen or as a tool. Tell the operator.
They will agree the steps with you, including what happens to the audit log,
because the log is meant to be a true record.

## Keep your own record

The software does not record requests for you. Your own notes are the record
until a screen exists.
