# Releasing decisions

Recording a decision and telling the applicant are two separate steps. This
page explains both, and what to do when an email does not go.

Two rules hold throughout:

- **A decision is private until it is released.** Recording an outcome tells
  nobody. Only staff can see it. An applicant sees a decision only after it
  is released.
- **A person decides.** The software never rejects, awards or pays on its own.
  Every outcome, and every release, is a named person's action.

## Who can do this

Recording and releasing decisions belongs to the **Programme manager** role.
The **Administrator** role does not include it. If you are an administrator
and need to release decisions, ask another administrator to add the
**Programme manager** role to your account. See
[Team and roles](team-and-roles.md).

Anyone without the role sees "You cannot record decisions" or "You cannot
release decisions".

## Record decisions

1. Open your programme, then the round, then choose **Decisions**.
2. Choose a submission and then **Record decision**.
3. Choose the **Outcome**: Accepted, Waitlisted or Declined.
4. For Accepted, enter the **Amount to award**. The form shows the range the
   round allows.
5. Choose **Record decision**.

The page says "Decision recorded for" the organisation's name, "Only staff can
see it." The row shows **Not released**.

To record the same outcome for several submissions, tick them and choose
**Record decisions**. You can do up to 200 at once. They all succeed or none
do.

The page shows a summary and **Awarded against budget**. If your awards add up
to more than the round's budget, it warns you. It is a warning. You decide what
to do.

Until you release, you can change a decision with **Change decision**. Every
recorded or changed decision is in the [audit log](audit-log.md).

A notice at the top reads "Applicants see nothing until you release decisions."

## Release decisions

1. On the **Decisions** page, choose **Release decisions**.
2. Read **What will happen**: how many awards, waitlisted and declined
   applicants, and how many emails.
3. Read **Checks**. They tell you whether the awards are within budget and
   whether every submission has a decision. Submissions with no decision are
   left out. Their applicants are told nothing.
4. Use **Preview the email for** to read the email each outcome sends.
5. Tick "I have checked the summary and the email..." to confirm.
6. Choose **Release decisions**.
7. A box titled "Confirm it is you" appears. Enter your password and the
   current code from your authenticator app.

The page then says "Decisions released" and counts the emails sent. Applicants
see their outcome when they sign in to the portal, and get an email.

You are always asked to confirm it is you when you release, even if you signed
in a moment ago. If you cancel the box, nothing is released.

### After you release

A released decision cannot be changed. Check before you confirm.

Releasing writes an event in the [audit log](audit-log.md) for the release.

## Find and resend emails that did not go

Emails go out straight after you confirm. Some may fail, for example because
of a mistyped address or a problem with the mail service.

On the **Decisions** page, each released row shows the email's state:
"Waiting to send", "Sent" or "Not sent".

If any email has not gone, a notice at the top of the **Release decisions**
page says, for example, "2 decision emails not sent". It explains that
applicants can already read their decision when they sign in, but have not had
the email yet.

1. Choose **Send unsent emails**.
2. If you signed in more than five minutes ago, enter your password and code in
   the "Confirm it is you" box.
3. The page says "Emails sent" and how many went and how many could not.

Each press sends up to 500 emails. Press again if more are waiting. If an email
still cannot go, check that the applicant's record has a correct email address,
then send again.

Resending sends an email only for a decision that has already been released.
It never releases anything.
