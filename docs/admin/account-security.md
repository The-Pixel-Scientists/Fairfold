# Account security

Use **Account security** to manage how you sign in. It is for you, not for the
whole team. Every team member can open it. You do not need the
**Administrator** role.

To open it, choose **Account security** in the account controls in the main
navigation. The page has five sections: **Passkeys**, **Authenticator app**,
**Backup codes**, **Email address** and **Password**.

## Confirm it is you first

Every change on this page asks you to confirm it is you. If you signed in more
than five minutes ago, a box titled "Confirm it is you" appears. Enter your
password and the current code from your authenticator app. The change then goes
ahead. If you cancel, nothing changes.

## Change your password

1. In **Password**, enter a new password. It needs at least 12 characters. A
   few words together is easy to remember and hard to guess.
2. Choose **Change password**.
3. Confirm it is you if asked.

The page says your password has changed. Anywhere else you were signed in is
signed out. This is the way to sign out your other sessions. There is no list
of sessions to choose from yet.

## Change your email address

1. In **Email address**, enter the **New email address**.
2. Choose **Change email address**.
3. Open the link we send to the new address.

Your address changes only when you open the link. The page gives the same
answer whether or not the address can be used.

## Replace your authenticator app

Use this when you have a new phone.

1. In **Authenticator app**, choose **Replace authenticator app**.
2. In your new app, add an account with the key shown. Enter the code it shows
   under **Code from the new app**.
3. Choose **Confirm new app**.

If the code is wrong, your old app still works. When the new app is confirmed,
you are signed out everywhere, including on this device. Choose **Sign in
again**.

To remove the app, choose **Remove authenticator app** and confirm. You can
only remove it if you have another way to sign in. Removing it also signs you
out everywhere.

If you have lost your authenticator app, use a backup code at the code prompt.
If you have none, ask the operator.

## Backup codes

Backup codes let you sign in if you lose your authenticator app or passkey.
Each works once. The page shows how many you have left.

1. Choose **Generate backup codes**, or **Generate new backup codes** if you
   already have some.
2. Copy the codes somewhere safe, such as a password manager.
3. Say you have saved them. The codes are not shown again.

New codes replace the old ones.

## Passkeys

A passkey signs you in with your fingerprint, face or screen lock instead of a
code.

1. In **Passkeys**, enter a **Name for the passkey**, for example `Work laptop`.
2. Choose **Add a passkey** and approve the prompt on your device.

If your browser cannot make passkeys, the page says so and does not show the
form. Use another browser.

To remove a passkey, choose its remove button and confirm. You can no longer
sign in with it.

## What the operator does

You cannot reset another person's password or authenticator app. The operator
does that. See [Team and roles](team-and-roles.md#password-and-authenticator-app-resets).
