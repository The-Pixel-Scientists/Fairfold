# Modules

A module is one of the tools your funder can use, such as Grants. No module is
on until it is switched on. Nobody at your funder can use a module that is off,
however their role is set.

You need the **Administrator** role. Choose **Settings**, then **Modules**.

## See what is on

Under **Available modules**, each module shows "is on" or "is off", with a
line saying what it does. Grants covers programmes, forms, applications,
reviews and decisions. The list also shows the other tools in the Fairfold
suite. Each tool's own guide describes its screens.

Organisations and people, and the platform itself, are always on. They are not
in the list.

## Switch a module on

1. Choose **Settings**, then **Modules**.
2. Find the module and choose **Switch on** followed by its name, for example
   **Switch on Grants**.
3. The page says "Grants is now on." and the navigation updates.

## Switch a module off

1. Choose **Switch off** followed by the module's name.
2. The page asks "Switch off Grants?" Choose **Switch off Grants** to confirm,
   or **Keep Grants on** to cancel.

While a module is off:

- its screens are hidden from the navigation for everyone at your funder;
- its pages and addresses are not found, for staff and for applicants;
- people lose the permissions that belong to it;
- nothing is deleted. Its data stays, and everything returns when you switch
  the module on again.

## Every switch is recorded

Each switch writes a configuration version and an event in the
[audit log](audit-log.md): "Module switched on" or "Module switched off".
Switching does not ask you to confirm it is you again.

## Who decides at the start

When the operator creates your funder, they choose which modules are on. If
they chose none, every module is off until you switch it on here.

If your funder existed before this rule, the operator ran a one-off step that
switched Grants on for it. If you find Grants off after an upgrade, switch it
on here, or ask the operator.

## If you cannot see Modules

Only people with the **Administrator** role can change modules. Anyone else
sees "You cannot change this funder's settings".
