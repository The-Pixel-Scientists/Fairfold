# Fairfold Payments

**Planned: work is due to start in 2027.** Nothing is built here yet.

Approved grant payments, donations and money owed, sent through regulated
payment providers and into your accounting system. It will not hold a ledger
or card details.

Fairfold Payments will be a module of the Fairfold suite, sharing its record
of organisations and people, its accounts and permissions, and its data
warehouse. See the [project README](../../README.md) for the whole suite and
what is built today.

Code name: `finance`.
