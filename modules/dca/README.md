# Fairfold Activities

**Planned: work is due to start in 2027.** Nothing is built here yet.

A charity's own direct charitable activities, recorded beside its grants in
the terms of the charity SORP, with procurement, contracts and support for
grantees.

Fairfold Activities will be a module of the Fairfold suite, sharing its record
of organisations and people, its accounts and permissions, and its data
warehouse. See the [project README](../../README.md) for the whole suite and
what is built today.

Code name: `dca`.
