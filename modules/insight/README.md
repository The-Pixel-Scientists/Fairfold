# Fairfold Reporting

**Planned: work is due to start in 2028.** Nothing is built here yet.

Reports for trustees and regulators from the shared data warehouse.

Fairfold Reporting will be a module of the Fairfold suite, sharing its record
of organisations and people, its accounts and permissions, and its data
warehouse. See the [project README](../../README.md) for the whole suite and
what is built today.
