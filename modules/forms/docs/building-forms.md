# Build a form

You build a form in the console under **Forms**. The builder is the one grant
applications use, so some of its words say "applicants" and "reviewers". In a
form, read "applicant" as "the person answering".

## Create a form

1. Choose **Forms**, then **Create form**.
2. Enter a **Form title**. People see it at the top of the form.
3. Choose **Create form**. The builder opens with an empty draft.

A new form is for people who sign in, with no opening or closing time. You
change that in [Form settings](publishing.md).

To start from a ready-made form, choose **Start from a template** instead. See
[Feedback templates](feedback-templates.md).

## Add sections and questions

A form has sections, and each section has questions.

1. On the **Edit form** tab, choose **Add section** and give it a title. An
   introduction is optional.
2. Choose **Add question**. Enter the **Question**, and an optional **Hint**.
3. Choose the **Type**. The text under the box says what the person does.
4. Tick **Applicants must answer this question** to make it required.
5. Use the move and remove buttons on a question or section to change the
   order. Removing a section asks you to confirm, because its questions go
   with it.

The types are short text, long text, number, amount in pounds, date, email
address, phone number, web address, choose one, choose one from a list, choose
several, yes or no, UK address, table and file upload. A form can also hold
plain text for people to read.

Field ids are generated by the builder. You never type one, and an id does not
change when you reword or move a question. Answers are stored against these
ids.

## Choose who sees each answer

Each question has a **Privacy** section with two controls.

**How sensitive is this answer?** has four levels:

| Level            | Meaning                                                       |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Public           | Fine for anyone to see, such as a project title.              |
| Internal         | For the foundation and its reviewers, and not about a person. |
| Personal         | About a named person.                                         |
| Special category | Sensitive details, such as health or ethnicity.               |

**Who sees the answer** has five choices: **Staff and reviewers**, **Staff
only, not reviewers**, **Reviewers only, not staff**, **The applicant only**
and **Only in totals**.

What this means in a form:

- The person answering can always see their own answer.
- Staff read responses through the answers their choice allows. **Staff only,
  not reviewers** and **Staff and reviewers** both show staff the answer.
  Forms has no reviewer screens.
- **Only in totals** means staff never see the answer on a response. A
  special category question must be set to **Only in totals**, and a question
  set to **Only in totals** must be special category. The builder refuses the
  draft otherwise. See [Responses and totals](responses-and-totals.md) for
  what that means for reports today.
- A form that anyone can answer cannot have an **Only in totals** question.
  See [Publish a form](publishing.md).
- Mark free text that may name people as **Personal**. The classification
  decides what may leave the platform; see
  [What reaches the warehouse](warehouse-data-contract.md).

The builder also shows **Identity field** and eligibility settings. They come
from grant applications. Forms does not use blind review, and nothing in forms
turns people away because of an eligibility answer, so leave them alone.

## Show or skip a question

Under **Show this question only when…**, choose **Add condition**.

1. Under **Earlier question**, pick a question that comes before this one.
   Only yes or no questions and the three choice types (choose one, choose one
   from a list, choose several) can be used.
2. Under **Answer is** (or **Answer includes** for choose several), pick the
   answer that makes this question appear.
3. Add more conditions if you need them. Every condition must hold for the
   question to show.

Plain text blocks take conditions the same way. The engine also accepts
conditions on a whole section, but the builder offers them only on questions
and text.

What the engine does:

- A condition can only read a question that comes before it in the form. A
  condition that reads a later question, or an answer the question cannot
  have, is refused when you save.
- Everyone who can see a question must also be able to see the questions that
  decide whether it shows, so a condition never gives away an answer.
- An answer to a hidden question counts as no answer. If someone answers a
  question and then changes an earlier answer so that it is hidden, the
  answering page does not send the hidden answer, and the server refuses any
  answer to a question that was not shown.
- A hidden question is never required.

Worked example. A form for a food bank asks "Did you collect a food parcel
this month?" (yes or no). A second question, "How could we make collecting a
parcel easier?", has the condition **Earlier question** "Did you collect a food
parcel this month?" and **Answer is** Yes. Someone who answers No never sees
the second question.

## Save the draft and preview it

Choose **Save draft**. The page shows "Saving…", then "Saved at" with the
time. The builder does not save a draft that has a problem, such as a question
with no text. It shows each problem next to its question and in a summary at
the top. It warns you if you leave with unsaved changes.

The **Preview** tab shows the saved draft as the server would show it. **Preview
as** offers **A person answering** and **Staff reading responses**. The tab
says "Preview only. Answers are not saved." and stores nothing. It shows the
saved draft, so save first; if you have unsaved changes it says "Save the
draft to preview it".

You can also import a form from a file with the import control. Save the draft
afterwards to keep it.

## Rules that apply

- A form's settings are configuration. Each change is kept with its author and
  what changed.
- Another funder cannot see or change your forms.
