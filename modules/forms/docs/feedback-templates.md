# Feedback templates

Three templates start a form for feedback from the people you support. Find
them under **Forms**, then **Start from a template**.

| Template                            | Purpose                                                         | Questions |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| Feedback on a service               | Ask people what they thought of a service you run or fund.      | 4         |
| Feedback after an event             | Ask people who came to an event what worked and what to change. | 4         |
| Check in on how things have changed | Ask people how your support has made a difference to them.      | 4         |

Each has one section. Each has a rating question ("Very good" to "Very poor"),
open comment questions, and one more choice or yes or no question. The comment
questions are "Personal" because people may name themselves or others. The
others are "Internal".

## Use a template

1. Choose **Preview template** on the one you want. The page shows every
   question. It says "Preview only. Answers are not saved."
2. Under **Make a form from this template**, check or change the **Form
   title**. It starts as the template's name.
3. Choose **Use this template**. The builder opens with a draft.

The template is copied. Changing your form never changes the template, and a
later change to a template never changes your form. You can change, add,
remove and reorder questions as in any draft. See
[Build a form](building-forms.md).

The new form is for people who sign in, and it is not published. To collect
anonymous feedback, open **Form settings**, choose **Anyone**, then publish.
No template asks for files or special category answers, so every template can
be published for anyone to answer.

## Equality questions

None of the three templates asks an equality or monitoring question. If you
add one, mark it **Special category** and **Only in totals**. Keep it that way:
the builder refuses to publish a lowered sensitivity, and a form open to anyone
cannot hold an **Only in totals** question. Put equality questions on a form
for people who sign in.
