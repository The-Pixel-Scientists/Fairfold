# Duplicate flags

A duplicate flag says that two records look alike, so a person should take a
look. That is all it says. It does not say the records are the same, and it
never changes anything.

## What a flag never does

- It never blocks or delays a submission.
- It never rejects, scores or changes an application.
- It never links or merges records.
- It is never shown to the applicant.

A flag holds two record ids and the reason it was raised. It holds no names,
emails or phone numbers.

## The three reasons

The panel says why each flag was raised.

| The panel says                              | What it means                                               |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Shares a verified identifier with another record | Two records have the same identifier, and staff have verified it |
| Has a similar name to another record        | Two records have names, and sometimes postcodes or organisations, that are alike |
| Shares a contact email or phone with another record | Two records give the same contact email or phone       |

A number someone has only typed, such as a charity number nobody has
verified, never raises a flag. Typing a number never links anyone.

## See the possible duplicates for an application

You need "Review and dismiss duplicate application flags".

1. Open the application's **Due diligence** page.
2. Read the **Possible duplicates** panel. It says "Flags are advisory" and
   lists each flag with **Why it was flagged** and when it was **Raised**.
3. Choose **Review in duplicates** to look at the two records in the CRM.
   There you decide whether they are the same.

Where there are none, the panel says "No possible duplicates found for this
application."

Flags are worked out when you open the panel, from the records as they are
then. If you change a record, the flags change with it.

## Dismiss a flag

Dismissing a flag with a reason is not available yet. The panel has no
**Dismiss flag** button, and the software does not store a dismissal. A flag
that is still true appears each time you open the panel.

## Merging records

Merging two records is done in the CRM, not here. Fairfold Due Diligence only
points to the records.

## What is not checked

Reused bank details are not checked in this release.
