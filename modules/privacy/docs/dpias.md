# Impact assessments

Use this guide to run a data protection impact assessment (DPIA). You need the
`privacy.dpias.manage` permission. UK GDPR Article 35 requires a DPIA before
processing that is likely to be a high risk to people. Confirm when one is
needed with your own data protection adviser.

## Decide whether you need one

The **Screening questions** on a new DPIA help you decide. They ask whether the
processing:

- uses special category or criminal offence data on a large scale;
- involves children or other vulnerable people;
- monitors people systematically;
- profiles people or makes automated decisions about them;
- uses new technology;
- matches or combines data from different sources;
- collects data in a way people may not expect or cannot easily avoid;
- could stop people getting a grant or another service.

Answer each with Yes, No or Not sure. The tool does not score the answers.
Several Yes answers are a strong sign you need a full assessment. Keep a
Not sure answer as a reason to ask your adviser.

## Start a DPIA

1. Open **Data Protection**, then **DPIAs**.
2. Choose to add a DPIA. The list is titled **Data protection impact
   assessments**.
3. Under **About this assessment**, enter a **Title** and choose the
   processing activities it covers. Add the activities first in
   [Records of processing](records-of-processing.md); a DPIA links to them by
   record, not by typing a name.
4. Answer the screening questions.
5. Under **Risks**, add each risk. Enter **The risk**, **How likely** (Remote,
   Possible or Probable), **How severe** (Minimal, Significant or Severe) and
   **How you will reduce it**.
6. Under **Advice from your data protection officer**, write the advice you
   received, if you have a data protection officer.
7. Save. A new DPIA is a draft. Set **Status** to Draft or In review as work
   moves on.

If you leave with unsaved changes the page asks "Leave this page?"

The processors a DPIA covers come through its processing activities. Each
activity lists its processors.

## Sign off

Choose **Sign off DPIA**. You sign in your own name. Enter **Review on**: the
day the assessment should be looked at again. It must be after today.

Signing off records who signed and when. Nobody else can sign for you.

If you change a signed-off DPIA and save, it reopens as a draft and needs
signing off again.

## Review dates

The register shows each DPIA's status: Draft, In review, Signed off or Review
due. A signed-off DPIA should become Review due when its review date comes. At
the time of writing the software does not do this on its own, and sends no
reminder. Look at the review dates in the register each month.

## Copy a template

[DPIA template](templates/dpia.md) is a plain document you can copy for your
own records. Its sections match the screens above. If you host the software
yourself, your operator's own DPIA for the platform is separate. This tool
holds yours for your funder's processing.

## What is recorded

Creating, changing, signing off and opening a DPIA each write an audit event
with ids and codes only. Screening answers and risks are personal data. The
advice is special category text.
