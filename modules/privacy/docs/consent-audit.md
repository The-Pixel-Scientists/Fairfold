# Consent history

Use this guide to see every consent choice a person has made. You need the
`privacy.requests.manage` permission. It helps when a person asks what they
agreed to, or when you check a complaint about contact.

The history comes from the organisations and people records, where consent is
kept as the master record. This tool only reads it. It cannot change consent.

## Read a person's consent history

1. Open the person's request under **Data Protection**, then **Requests**.
2. In **Details**, find **Consent history** and choose **View consent
   history**.
3. Read the table, newest first.

Each row shows:

- **Date**: when the choice was recorded, in your funder's time zone;
- **Purpose**: what the person agreed to be contacted about;
- **Channel**: how, such as email;
- **Choice**: "Gave consent" or "Withdrew consent";
- **Privacy notice version**: the version of the notice the person was shown.

If the person has made no choices, the page says "No consent choices recorded".

Opening the history writes an audit event, because it is a read of personal
data. Consent is one lawful basis among several. A person with no consent
recorded is not necessarily being contacted unlawfully. Ask your adviser.
